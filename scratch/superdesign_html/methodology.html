Source: Give Me Some Credit (Kaggle)

Historical financial records of borrowers used to train models to identify credit risk.

Target Variable

SeriousDlqin2yrs

  • 0 = No serious delinquency
  • 1 = Serious delinquency within 2 years

Preprocessing

  • Missing-value handling: Imputation techniques for MonthlyIncome and NumberOfDependents.
  • Duplicate handling: Removal of exact duplicate records to prevent data leakage.
  • Extreme-value treatment: Capping/winsorizing extreme outliers in revolving utilization and debt ratio.
  • Feature engineering: Creation of derived financial features.
  • Train/Test split: Stratified splitting to maintain target class distribution.
